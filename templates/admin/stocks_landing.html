What is the NNS Score?

NNS Score is our own 0-to-10 measure of how strong a stock looks right now, built from 10 equally-weighted, quantified checks -- valuation (PE, PEG, price-to-book against its own industry), profitability (OPM, ROCE, ROA), growth (profit and revenue), promoter/FII holding trend, and technical positioning (RSI, moving-average crossover). Nothing subjective, nothing hidden -- every point on the score traces back to a real number.

Golden -- 8.0 and above Silver -- 6.0 and above Bronze -- 4.0 and above

Built for how you actually trade

These are equity research picks, not futures/options calls. Some picks suit a quick swing, others suit holding for the long term -- the reasoning we send tells you which is which and why.

Swing trades

When a chart pattern (like a rounding bottom or a breakout) is behind the call, you get a specific buy price and target for a shorter move -- with the historical reliability of that exact pattern cited alongside it.

Short to medium term

Picks built mainly on technical positioning (a fresh golden cross, momentum turning) are flagged as such, so you know it's a shorter conviction window, not a buy-and-forget call.

Long term / core equity

Picks driven mainly by fundamentals -- strong, improving financials at a reasonable valuation -- are the ones worth researching for a core, long-hold portfolio position.
