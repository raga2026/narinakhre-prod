How the process works

  1. Fetch daily prices for each driver (Brent crude, natural gas, copper, USD/INR, US 10-year yield, India VIX) and flag a shock when a move is unusually large against its own recent volatility.
  2. News headlines are pulled per sector and per driver and scored for sentiment.
  3. Pressure per sector (-1 to +1) blends driver moves (50%), news sentiment (25%) and the sector's own recent price move (25%). Below -0.5 new buy picks in that sector are paused.
  4. Effect on picks: pressure adjusts each stock's NNS score (up to -1.0 for headwinds, +0.25 for tailwinds).
  5. Alerts: a driver shock emails the owner with the exposed watchlist companies.
