What is the StoqBell Score?

StoqBell Score is our own 0-to-10 measure of how strong a stock looks right now, built from 10 equally-weighted, quantified checks -- valuation (PE, PEG, price-to-book against its own industry), profitability (OPM, ROCE, ROA), growth (profit and revenue), promoter/FII holding trend, and technical positioning (RSI, moving-average crossover). Nothing subjective, nothing hidden -- every point on the score traces back to a real number.

Golden -- 8.0 and above Silver -- 6.0 and above Bronze -- 4.0 and above
